About

The Sussex PGR Workshop in Economics is an internal one-day event organised by PhD researchers, with support from the Department of Economics. This is its first edition.

Its aim is to strengthen the research community and research environment in the department. The workshop gives visibility to the research done by PGRs at Sussex, and creates a space to present work in progress, receive constructive feedback from peers and faculty, and learn what colleagues are working on.

We welcome work from all fields of economics, including applied and theoretical micro, macroeconomics, development, labour, education, health, environmental and public economics, econometrics and economic history. Researchers at all stages of the PhD are encouraged to participate. Papers do not need to be publication-ready: a sufficiently developed research question, an empirical or theoretical approach and preliminary results are enough to benefit from discussion.

Programme

Eight papers in three sessions: public economics, taxation and governance; economics of education, inequality and well-being; and macroeconomics, finance and international trade. Each paper is followed by comments from a faculty discussant and open questions.

Call for papers

Submissions are now closed. Thank you to everyone who submitted.

Who can apply

PGRs in the Department of Economics, University of Sussex. Subject to capacity, PhD students working on economics-related topics across the University are also welcome to apply. Co-authored papers are welcome, as long as the presenter meets these criteria.

What to submit

  • Paper title, presenter name, department and email.
  • One PDF with your full paper or an extended abstract.

Format

Each accepted paper gets a presentation slot followed by comments from a discussant and open questions.
